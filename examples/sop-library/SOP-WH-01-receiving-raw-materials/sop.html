1. Purpose

Make sure every delivery of tea leaves and packaging is checked against the order and for quality before it enters stock, so we never pay for, store or use goods that are wrong, short or unsafe.

2. Scope

Applies to: all deliveries of raw materials (tea leaves, matcha powder, packaging) at the Acme Tea warehouse, during opening hours 08:00-16:00. Does not cover: purchase ordering (SOP-PU-01), stock opname (SOP-WH-02), deliveries of finished goods to customers.
