Purpose

Keep every office and unit area of Acme Tea clean, safe and working, with every finding followed up within a clear time limit.

Scope

Applies to: daily inspection of office and unit areas (lobby, workspaces, toilets, pantry, warehouse, outdoor areas), from inspection until every finding is closed. Does not cover: scheduled maintenance of machines and building (SOP-ENG-01), purchasing cleaning supplies (SOP-GA-03).

Procedure

Trigger: control round at 08:00 and 14:00 every working day. Output: every finding is closed and the checklist is filed.

ResponsibleNo.ActivityDocument
GA staff6.1Start the control round on schedule, taking checklist F-01 and a phone for photos.If unavailable, the GA manager assigns a replacement. · 08:00 and 14:00
GA staff6.2Inspect each area in the order of F-01: floors, toilets, pantry, bins, air conditioning, lights and building condition.Use the "Standard" column on F-01, not personal judgement. · 30 min
GA staff6.3Mark each item OK or Not OK and photograph every finding.Control Every "Not OK" needs a photo and a location. · During inspectionF-01
GA staff6.4Decide the type of finding. Cleaning: go to 6.5. Technical (AC, lights, building): go to 6.8. Both: do 6.5 and 6.8. None: go to 6.11.
GA staff6.5Ask the CS supervisor to re-clean the area, giving the location and the finding.Within 15 minF-01 follow-up column
Cleaning service6.6Re-clean the area according to the finding, then report back to GA staff.Within 60 min
GA staff6.7Re-inspect the area and mark it "Done" on F-01.If it still fails the standard, repeat step 6.5 once, then escalate to the GA manager. · After the CS reportF-01
GA staff6.8Raise work order F-02, sign it and hand it to the engineering team.Control One technical finding = one numbered work order. · Same dayF-02
Engineering team6.9Repair the finding as described in the work order, then record the action and finish time on F-02.Urgent: leaks, electrical faults, safety risks. · Urgent 4 h; normal 3 working daysF-02
GA staff6.10Check the repair, then close the work order with a signature.Same working day as the repair reportF-02 closed
GA staff6.11Hand the completed F-01 to the GA manager and file it.Control The GA manager initials the daily checklist. · Daily at 16:00F-01 filed
